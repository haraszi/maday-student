The tool I use

What is DISC, and why does it help?

DISC is a well-established behavioural profile. It describes how you tend to act, communicate and respond to challenges, and what motivates you. It doesn't put you in a box. It gives you a language for yourself.

D

Dominance

Driven by results and challenge. Likes to decide, lead and move fast.

I

Influence

Energised by people and ideas. Persuades, inspires and builds connections.

S

Steadiness

Values trust, stability and teamwork. Patient, loyal and a great supporter.

C

Conscientiousness

Loves accuracy, quality and logic. Thinks things through and gets them right.

Everyone is a blend of all four. Your particular mix says a lot about the work environments where you'll thrive, and the ones that will quietly drain you. That is exactly what we use to make better career decisions.

Questions

Things students often ask me

Is DISC a personality test I can pass or fail?

Not at all. There are no good or bad profiles. DISC simply describes your natural style, and every style has its own strengths.

Will it tell me exactly which job to do?

No tool can do that, and I'd be wary of any that claims to. What DISC does is show you which kinds of roles and environments suit you, so your choices become much clearer. The decision stays yours.

I'm in my final year. Is it too late?

Never. Whether you're choosing a degree, a master's, your first job or a change of direction, knowing yourself better helps at every stage.

Do we meet in person or online?

Sessions are held online, so you can join from wherever you're studying.
