Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect when you use maday.ae, why I collect it, who I share it with, and your rights. I've tried to keep it short and in plain words. If anything is unclear, email me at hello@maday.ae.

1. Who I am

maday.ae is run by Ildi Haraszi, a DISC certified career coach based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates ([trade licence name and number, if you have one]). In this policy, "I", "me" and "maday.ae" mean Ildi Haraszi. You can contact me at hello@maday.ae.

2. The free quiz

The free Career Style Compass quiz runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and your result are not sent to me or stored anywhere. When you close the page, they're gone. You don't need to give a name or email to take the quiz.

4. The legal basis for using your data

  • To deliver what you bought: I need your name, email, language and order details to provide the assessment, report and debrief.
  • Your consent: for your answers to the three questions, and for a parent or guardian's consent for under-18s. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing me.
  • Legal obligations: I may need to keep order records for accounting and tax purposes.

I handle your data in line with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

6. How long I keep your data

I keep your order details, your answers and your contact details for [24 months] after your purchase, so I can help if you have questions or want a follow-up debrief. After that I delete them, unless the law requires me to keep order records for longer. You can ask me to delete your data earlier at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what personal data I hold about you and send you a copy
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your data
  • stop using your data for a particular purpose

Email hello@maday.ae and I'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Ziina, Cal.com and Cloudflare may use essential cookies on their own pages to keep payments, bookings and the website secure.

9. Security

The website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection. Your data is only shared with the services listed above, and access to it is limited to me.

10. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, I'll tell customers by email.
